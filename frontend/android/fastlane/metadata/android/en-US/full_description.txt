Nokia Launcher turns an Android phone into a calmer, more deliberate tool.

Inspired by the clarity and dependable interaction of classic keypad phones, it replaces the conventional launcher with a focused home screen, tactile navigation, finite menus, and a refined monochrome interface.

This is more than a retro visual theme. The goal is to preserve useful modern capabilities while reducing the noise and compulsive patterns that make smartphones difficult to put down.

Modern capability without modern-phone addiction.

WHAT EACH FUNCTION IS FOR

- Home screen: Keeps only essential actions visible, helping you begin with a purpose instead of browsing.
- Clock: Shows the time immediately without opening another app.
- Phone and direct dialing: Supports the phone's most fundamental job—making a call quickly.
- Messages: Keeps intentional person-to-person communication close.
- Maps: Preserves modern navigation without placing distracting content on the home screen.
- Built-in camera: Captures photos and videos and provides a local gallery within the same focused interface.
- Search: Provides deliberate access to Google when information is genuinely needed.
- App drawer: Keeps installed apps reachable through a finite list instead of filling the home screen.
- Keypad and D-pad: Provide predictable navigation, familiar muscle memory, and a clear relationship between input and action.
- Multi-tap app search: Finds apps without requiring a full on-screen keyboard.
- Settings shortcuts: Open the relevant Android controls for display, sound, keyboard, Digital Wellbeing, and privacy.
- Live status area: Shows useful network, connectivity, sound, notification, charging, power-saving, and battery information without the original Android status bar.

BUILT FOR CALMER USE

Nokia Launcher favors utility over engagement. It avoids infinite feeds, autoplay, streaks, algorithmic recommendations, and attention-seeking animation. The interface is stable, fast, easy to understand, and easy to leave after completing a task.

The launcher supports touch and its built-in Nokia-style keypad controls. Modern apps remain accessible, but they do not dominate the default experience.

PRIVACY-MINDED BY DESIGN

Core launcher behavior is designed around local device actions and minimal data handling. Permissions are requested only when required for features such as taking photos, recording video audio, placing a call, showing installed apps, or reading device status. Camera media remains in the device's local media storage.
