Questions, answered

Before you switch.

Is this a real phone?

No. Nokia Retro Phone is an Android launcher that gives your current phone a calmer, keypad-inspired interface.

Will my existing apps still work?

Yes. Essential apps remain available through a simple menu; the launcher changes how you reach them, not what your phone can run.

Does it track my activity?

The product is designed around privacy and minimal permissions. This early-access page collects only the email address you choose to submit.

When can I try it?

The Android beta is being prepared now. Join the early-access list and you’ll hear when a testing place opens.
